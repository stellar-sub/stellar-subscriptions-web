## [0.1.0] - 2024-01-01

### Added

- Subscription, plan, and registry contract bindings for interacting with the on-chain contracts.
- Wallet connection and management flows, including connecting, disconnecting, and switching accounts.
- Event history tracking to surface past subscription and plan-related on-chain events.
- Initial user interface providing views for browsing plans, managing subscriptions, and viewing event history.
